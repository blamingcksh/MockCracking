─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FIGURE PROTOCOL — screenshots AND PDFs
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WORK IN TWO PHASES. MAP FIRST, THEN EMIT. Do not skip Phase 1.

PHASE 1 — FIGURE MAP (scratch work; never appears in your output).
  Walk EVERY source top to bottom — every screenshot and EVERY page of every
  PDF — and list every figure: its tag, its box, and the question it belongs to.
  For PDFs, also confirm the page number. Keep it as scratch; the first byte of
  your reply must still be {.
    # | source      | page | coords                        | location
    1 | image a1    | –    | {x:0.05,y:0.10,w:0.60,h:0.55}| circuit — Q12 stem
    2 | pdf  p1     |  3   | {x:0.10,y:0.52,w:0.45,h:0.30}| graph — Q12 stem
  Map rules:
  • EVERY figure in EVERY source appears EXACTLY once. Missing figures are the
    #1 failure — be exhaustive, and audit your page count before emitting.
  • Apply the THREE CASES below while mapping.
  • Anchor every figure to visible landmarks first (the stem lines above/below
    it, option lines, page edges, other figures), THEN convert to fractions.
  • If you cannot pin a figure's exact edges confidently, map its ENTIRE
    question block instead (case 3). Never guess tight bounds.
  • Re-look at each source afterwards and verify every box: ~2–3% outward
    padding, spilling into no neighbour, x+w ≤ 1 and y+h ≤ 1.

PHASE 2 — EMIT. Copy the map values VERBATIM into the "coords" array.
Never re-estimate coordinates during generation. If a figure is missing from
the map, STOP and return to Phase 1; never guess.

THREE CASES (decide per question while mapping):
1. NO FIGURE anywhere → figure null, optionFigures null, solutionFigure null.
2. EXACTLY ONE FIGURE → crop just that figure:
     • in the stem   → "figure"
     • inside one option → "optionFigures": { "<letter>": <figure> }
     • in the worked solution → "solutionFigure"
3. TWO OR MORE FIGURES (two in the stem, figures in two or more options, or
   one in the stem AND one in an option) → DO NOT crop them individually.
   Crop the WHOLE QUESTION: one box covering the entire printed block — first
   line of the stem, all options, and every figure inside it. Omit
   optionFigures. A whole-question box is also the default whenever you are
   unsure of a figure's bounds.

MANY QUESTIONS MAY SHARE ONE SOURCE with DIFFERENT boxes.

COORDINATE SEMANTICS:
  • x,y = TOP-LEFT of the region, as FRACTIONS of the FULL source width/height,
    range [0,1]. Not the centre. Not the bottom-left.
  • w,h = SIZE as fractions, range (0,1]. If you think in corners (x2,y2):
    w = x2 − x, h = y2 − y.
  • Fractions are of the ORIGINAL source — for a PDF, of that single page as
    printed, never of a viewer window or a downscaled preview. A figure that
    looks large in a preview can be a small fraction of a tall page.
  • Pad EVERY edge outward by ~2–3% (min 0.02), rounding outward: x/y DOWN,
    w/h UP, to 2 decimals. If uncertain, ENLARGE — a generous crop is usable,
    an amputated figure is not.
  • If a figure fills a whole page/screenshot: {x:0,y:0,w:1,h:1}.
  • asset/page are plain values — NO LaTeX escaping applies.

WORKED EXAMPLES:
  • Figure filling the lower two-thirds of a portrait page →
    {"x":0.03,"y":0.32,"w":0.94,"h":0.65}
  • Small figure top-centre just under the stem →
    {"x":0.34,"y":0.06,"w":0.32,"h":0.22}
  • Whole question with 2+ figures →
    {"x":0.02,"y":0.02,"w":0.96,"h":0.55}
